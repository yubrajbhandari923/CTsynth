# F-050 · Physical-concentration U-Net reproduces simulated CT at 70 and unseen 80 keV and beats 70-keV voxelwise inverses, mostly by image restoration (simulated, 10 test patients)

## Statement

Simulated XCAT COPD data, 10 held-out test patients, 2 seeds. (1) A 3D U-Net trained with noise augmentation (arm C) predicts 8 composition-concentration maps whose body mu80 error (computed from the prediction) is 19.3 HU lower than the 2-composition HU-threshold baseline P1 on the pooled in-distribution panel (paired patient bootstrap CI -21.9 to -17.0; 10/10 patients) and 7.5 HU lower on clean input (CI -8.1 to -6.7; 10/10); the gap is already 19.5 HU (pooled) and 5.1 HU (clean) at mu70, so most of it is image restoration (denoising/deblurring toward the phantom, which voxelwise baselines reading the CT cannot do), not composition or energy inference (at most ~2.4 HU on clean input). On clean input the clean-trained arm A is better than C (+1.9 HU). (2) From clean-input predictions, passing predicted phantoms through LEAP reproduces the true-phantom CT at 70 keV (arm C body MAE 7.67 HU) and at the unseen 80 keV (7.52 HU; not larger than the 70 keV error; better than reusing the 70 keV image in 8 of 10 patients, 9.11 HU reference); all 10 patients passed the corrected guarded evaluation. (3) Arm D fails on the unseen pitch 1.4 (+129.9 HU vs C, all patients) because of conditioning-scalar extrapolation (constant-1 pitch input learnt as a bias), verified by an override experiment on validation patients; this is specific to the encoding, and on the pooled in-distribution panel C-B and D-C are not detected (D-C at pitch 1.4 is detected: +129.9 HU). (4) Skin and blood are not recovered by arm C (their mass goes to muscle/body); blood is not recovered by any arm, skin partly by one seed of arm D (D_s1). Not a claim of unique physical material recovery, DukeSim or clinical performance.

## Limitations

Simulated XCAT COPD data only, same LEAP simulator for inputs, targets and closed loop; 10 test patients, 2 seeds (bootstrap over patients excludes seed variance); closed loop from clean input only; cross-energy tested only 70->80 keV (80 keV is the only held-out energy; 60 keV is in var1); compositions not identifiable from CT intensity; conditioning failure is specific to un-centred zero-variance encoding and was not repaired (needs retraining); baselines read e60/var1 as 70 keV; RUN-0118 provenance: source tree clean at 863565e, whole-tree git_dirty from unrelated files; independent review F-050/R1 verdict needs_work addressed in this revision; re-review pending
