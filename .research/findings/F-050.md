# F-050 · Physical-concentration U-Net beats 70-keV-only physics inverses and reproduces the CT at 70 and unseen 80 keV (simulated, 10 test patients)

## Statement

On 10 held-out simulated XCAT COPD test patients, a 3D U-Net trained with noise augmentation (arm C) predicts 8 composition-concentration maps whose body mu80 error (computed from the prediction, HU-equivalent) is 19.3 HU lower than the 2-composition HU-threshold baseline P1 on the pooled in-distribution panel (paired patient bootstrap CI -21.9 to -17.0; 10/10 patients; floor 0.866 HU) and body density error is 0.0240 g/cm3 lower. Passing predicted phantoms through LEAP reproduces the true-phantom CT at 70 keV and at the unseen 80 keV (body MAE 6.3-7.7 HU and 6.2-7.5 HU) versus 9.1 HU for reusing the 70 keV image. Noise-trained vs clean-trained and conditioning effects: C-B and D-C not detected; arm D fails on unseen pitch 1.4 (+129.9 HU). Skin and blood concentrations are not recovered.

## Limitations

Simulated XCAT COPD data only (borrowed Duke1 geometry, monoenergetic, no scatter); 10 test patients, 2 seeds; targets from the generating phantoms; compositions not identifiable from CT, separation of air/lung/body/muscle/bone rests on anatomical priors; cross-energy tested only 70->80 keV; resim of 9 of 10 patients ran before the silent-FBP guard (consistent values, not rerun); preliminary until independent review
