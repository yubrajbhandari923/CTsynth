A FULLY NONNEGATIVE exact decoy exists at ||dx|| = 19.73 cm: truth [1.0, 14.0, 0.2] cm vs decoy [15.272, 0.372, 0.35] cm reproduces BOTH kVp transmissions with relative error exactly 0.0. All components are nonnegative, so no nonnegativity constraint rejects it. My earlier 6.53 cm example was badly chosen (it had fibroglandular = -0.51, i.e. physically unreachable and trivially excluded) and my band-pixel decoy at ||dx|| = 0.26 cm was an underestimate by ~75x. CORRECTED STATEMENT: nonnegativity does NOT bound the ambiguity at all in the physically reachable region; the exact-measurement decoy family contains physically admissible members separated by tens of cm of path length. This STRENGTHENS F-018's conclusion (consistency alone cannot identify) while invalidating my characterisation of what nonnegativity buys.
