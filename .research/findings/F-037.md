# F-037 · CORRECTED: spectrum specification is the largest CTsynth-vs-XCIST projection mismatch (about 2.4-3.2%, binning-convention dependent)

## Statement

Supersedes F-035 (review found a +-3 keV binning window double-counted photons at 80 kVp, whose XCIST bins are 4 keV wide). SYNTHETIC water/Ca/I disk phantoms, XCIST 1.6.9 noise-free single-ray projections, EI detector, no scatter. Relative RMS of delta(-ln T) on object rays, RUN-0023: with XCIST's own spectrum/detector/attenuation CTsynth reproduces XCIST to 0.47-1.05% (analytic chords) and 0.26-0.47% with voxel-marched chords (the adapter floor; shared transport equation, so a check not independent validation). Our spectra_v1, mapped to XCIST's bin edges by exact overlap weights, give 2.36% (G3 120 kVp) to 3.21% (G2 80 kVp) with systematic positive signed mean (our spectra are softer), the largest single term; a point-binned convention gives 2.09-2.70% and a half-bin edge shift 4-6%, so only 'about 2-3%+' is robust. Attenuation tables (xraylib vs Geant4): ~0.1-0.2% in projections. Detector response: <=0.25% relative (<=0.012 absolute in -ln units; sign depends on kVp; XCIST weights treated as reference). A reviewer independently recomputed M4 and confirmed the object-ray mask choice does not change conclusions.

## Limitations

Two different spectrum specifications, not an error of either; 1-keV spectra mapped onto a 4-6 keV quadrature (sensitivity +-0.5 pp binning, +-2 pp edge placement); XCIST is itself a model; 1-row 2D, disks, no scatter, no photon counting, no reconstruction-domain comparison. This superseding finding still needs its own independent review.
