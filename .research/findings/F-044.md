# F-044 · Stage 0: spatially structured adipose<->fibro errors are 6-14x less visible (RMSE; 38-210x in MSE) than a uniform swap and fall below the network residual; the four label-free priors cannot choose the sign

## Statement

RUN-0093 (20 train cases, noise-free, around the truth): projection MSE(t)=c t^2 with c = 1.6e-9 (exact null), 1.7e-4 (uniform swap), 4.5e-6 (smooth swap field), 8.0e-7 (random +-1 swap field). Smallest |t| exceeding a residual MSE of 2e-8 (best label-free refinement, EXP-028): uniform 0.011, smooth 0.067, rough 0.158; of 1e-6 (physics-only networks): 0.077, 0.47, 1.11. Calcification-absence (L1, smooth L0), bimodality and TV priors are exactly invariant under A<->F relabelling (change 0 to 1e-17); only a train-label marginal (flagged weak supervision) discriminates (dR 0.35). TV has no margin along the null direction (profile decreases); calc_l1 has no weight window at the network residual. Pre-registered Stage-0 gate: pass for calc_l0, calc_l1 (classical level), bimodal, w1_marginal; fail for tv_a; P1/bimodal pass partly by construction.

## Limitations

Local profiles around the truth with synthetic swap fields; train cases; zero-thickness null vector; MSE over all rays; lambda windows use train labels; unreviewed.
