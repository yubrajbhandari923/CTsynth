# F-007 · SUPERSEDES F-001. Identifiability separates into a structural part and an empirical part. STRUCTURAL (forced by matrix shape, not an experimental result): with n_meas measurements and k unknown materials, rank <= min(n_meas,k), so 1 energy-integrating measurement cannot separate 2+ materials and dual-kVp cannot separate 3. EMPIRICAL (the real content): among designs that ARE identifiable, conditioning differs substantially and is dose-independent - dual-kVp 2-material median condition number 195 (max 506), 4-bin photon counting 2-material 99, 4-bin 3-material 217. Rank verdicts are robust: smallest/largest singular-value ratios are 2e-3 to 1e-2, so any threshold from 1e-2 to 1e-10 gives identical ranks. Noise cost at equal total photons, SAME basis size (k=2, iodine CRLB): 4-bin 0.0019 vs dual-kVp 0.0047 g/cm2, i.e. photon counting is 2.5x BETTER. The previously quoted 2.70x 'worse' figure compared M4 k=3 against M2 k=2 and confounded design with basis size; it is the price of estimating a third material, not a penalty of photon counting.

## Statement

SUPERSEDES F-001. Identifiability separates into a structural part and an empirical part. STRUCTURAL (forced by matrix shape, not an experimental result): with n_meas measurements and k unknown materials, rank <= min(n_meas,k), so 1 energy-integrating measurement cannot separate 2+ materials and dual-kVp cannot separate 3. EMPIRICAL (the real content): among designs that ARE identifiable, conditioning differs substantially and is dose-independent - dual-kVp 2-material median condition number 195 (max 506), 4-bin photon counting 2-material 99, 4-bin 3-material 217. Rank verdicts are robust: smallest/largest singular-value ratios are 2e-3 to 1e-2, so any threshold from 1e-2 to 1e-10 gives identical ranks. Noise cost at equal total photons, SAME basis size (k=2, iodine CRLB): 4-bin 0.0019 vs dual-kVp 0.0047 g/cm2, i.e. photon counting is 2.5x BETTER. The previously quoted 2.70x 'worse' figure compared M4 k=3 against M2 k=2 and confounded design with basis size; it is the price of estimating a third material, not a penalty of photon counting.
