# F-012 · AAPM 3-material conditioning, computed from the official starting-kit spectra and mu tables (md5-verified kit, 10 sample cases): the spectrum-weighted effective attenuation matrix is [[0.26697,0.31321,3.01182],[0.21930,0.24788,1.64238]] 1/cm for (adipose,fibroglandular,calcification) at 50/80 kVp. Adipose and fibroglandular are spectrally near-degenerate: column cosine 0.999834, i.e. 1.04 degrees apart. The unconstrained 2x3 null direction is [-0.723,+0.691,-0.008], confirming F-010's prediction concretely: adipose can be traded against fibroglandular with essentially no measurement change. Appending the sum-to-one row makes the 3x3 system full rank (singular values 3.697, 1.176, 0.00290) but POORLY CONDITIONED at cond=1273. Therefore the partition constraint restores identifiability but error in the adipose/fibroglandular direction is amplified ~1000x; exact recovery in the noiseless challenge data is possible, while any model mismatch or interpolation error is magnified by that factor. Image-domain linear inversion is expected to be far worse than the published model-based winners (6.8e-7).

## Statement

AAPM 3-material conditioning, computed from the official starting-kit spectra and mu tables (md5-verified kit, 10 sample cases): the spectrum-weighted effective attenuation matrix is [[0.26697,0.31321,3.01182],[0.21930,0.24788,1.64238]] 1/cm for (adipose,fibroglandular,calcification) at 50/80 kVp. Adipose and fibroglandular are spectrally near-degenerate: column cosine 0.999834, i.e. 1.04 degrees apart. The unconstrained 2x3 null direction is [-0.723,+0.691,-0.008], confirming F-010's prediction concretely: adipose can be traded against fibroglandular with essentially no measurement change. Appending the sum-to-one row makes the 3x3 system full rank (singular values 3.697, 1.176, 0.00290) but POORLY CONDITIONED at cond=1273. Therefore the partition constraint restores identifiability but error in the adipose/fibroglandular direction is amplified ~1000x; exact recovery in the noiseless challenge data is possible, while any model mismatch or interpolation error is magnified by that factor. Image-domain linear inversion is expected to be far worse than the published model-based winners (6.8e-7).
