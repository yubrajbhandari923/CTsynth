# F-018 · EXPERIMENT A (deterministic, no training): a pure measurement-consistency loss CANNOT identify 3 material maps from 2 spectral measurements on the AAPM model, and this is not a linearisation artifact. Using the REAL polychromatic forward model and the official CC-BY spectra/mu tables, I solved for alternative material path-length vectors that reproduce BOTH kVp transmissions to machine precision: holding calcification at 0.05/0.06/0.08/0.10 cm and re-solving adipose+fibroglandular by Newton gives exact-measurement decoys with relative transmission error <= 1.8e-16 and ||dx|| up to 6.53 cm (e.g. truth [8.0,4.0,0.05] vs decoy [12.72,-0.51,0.10]). The decoy family is one-dimensional, as predicted by the 2x3 rank. Consequence: ANY label-free objective whose only signal is measurement consistency has a continuum of exact global minima; a network trained on it converges to whichever member its implicit priors (architecture, initialisation, amortisation) prefer, NOT to the truth. Reporting a low consistency loss as evidence of material accuracy is therefore invalid for this problem.

## Statement

EXPERIMENT A (deterministic, no training): a pure measurement-consistency loss CANNOT identify 3 material maps from 2 spectral measurements on the AAPM model, and this is not a linearisation artifact. Using the REAL polychromatic forward model and the official CC-BY spectra/mu tables, I solved for alternative material path-length vectors that reproduce BOTH kVp transmissions to machine precision: holding calcification at 0.05/0.06/0.08/0.10 cm and re-solving adipose+fibroglandular by Newton gives exact-measurement decoys with relative transmission error <= 1.8e-16 and ||dx|| up to 6.53 cm (e.g. truth [8.0,4.0,0.05] vs decoy [12.72,-0.51,0.10]). The decoy family is one-dimensional, as predicted by the 2x3 rank. Consequence: ANY label-free objective whose only signal is measurement consistency has a continuum of exact global minima; a network trained on it converges to whichever member its implicit priors (architecture, initialisation, amortisation) prefer, NOT to the truth. Reporting a low consistency loss as evidence of material accuracy is therefore invalid for this problem.
