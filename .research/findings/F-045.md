# F-045 · EXP-036 pilot (3 validation cases, noiseless, oracle occupancy S(x), calcification=0 arm A): per-case full-view L-BFGS reaches projection MSE 1.7e-9 (557-calcified-pixel case; 2.4e-8 and 3.2e-8 at 1062 and 1441) from five different inits to the same solution, clipped s1 0.31->0.014-0.048, but the >1/8 cycles/px adipose-fraction error stays 0.21/0.35/0.42 (criterion 0.05): convergence fixes the bulk map and does not recover the spatially varying A/F error.

## Statement

EXP-036 pilot (3 validation cases, noiseless, oracle occupancy S(x), calcification=0 arm A): per-case full-view L-BFGS reaches projection MSE 1.7e-9 (557-calcified-pixel case; 2.4e-8 and 3.2e-8 at 1062 and 1441); five inits agree to 4 digits in MSE and 3 in band error (same basin, not a proof of uniqueness). Clipped s1 falls 0.31->0.014-0.048, but UNCLIPPED s1 is 0.209/0.355/0.435. The reported >1/8 cycles/px adipose error (0.21/0.35/0.42) is computed on UNCLIPPED a and is probably dominated by out-of-[0,1] excursions near calcifications (clipping removes 98-99.5% of the squared s1 error); it is NOT evidence that the spatially varying A/F error is unrecovered. A clipped / away-from-lesion version of the metric is not yet computed.

## Limitations

3 validation cases; sum known via oracle S; arm A cannot represent lesions; hf metric unclipped (review 2026-10-19); misspecification vs non-identifiability unresolved though B-uniform hf 0.035-0.037 (no growth with lesions) leans to lesion/excursion-driven; float32; numbers from A-0370..A-0375, not evidence-bound
