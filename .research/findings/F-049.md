# F-049 · Varied-setting training generalises to unseen noise, kernel and photon energy; metadata conditioning adds nothing detectable (simulated COPD CT, 10 test patients)

## Statement

On 10 unseen test patients, clean-only training (A) reaches macro Dice 0.917 on clean CT but 0.691 at N0=1e5 noise and 0.670 with a smoother kernel; noise-aware (B) and varied (C, D) training keep 0.905-0.911 on clean/noisy CT; only varied training (C 0.892, D 0.893) also holds under the smoother kernel (B 0.716); at held-out 80 keV all arms degrade (A 0.736, B 0.800, C 0.840, D 0.848), mostly bone classes. FiLM conditioning on acquisition metadata (D vs C) shows -0.0007 [-0.0015,0.0000] in distribution and +0.0084 [0.0051,0.0121] at 80 keV, within the seed spread and below the pre-registered detection floor, so no conditioning benefit is claimed. All arms beat an intensity-only baseline (0.20-0.27) by >= 0.62. Scope: synthetic data; 15 organ-group labels; not about material composition.

## Limitations

Simulated CT, 10 test patients, 2 seeds, two held-out acquisition settings, labels from the generating phantoms, N0 not calibrated; no independent review yet.
