# F-035 · On XCIST-generated known-material disk phantoms, spectrum specification dominates the CTsynth-vs-XCIST projection mismatch

## Statement

SYNTHETIC water/Ca/I disk phantoms (3 geometries x 80,120 kVp), XCIST 1.6.9 noise-free single-ray projections as reference, energy-integrating detector, no scatter. Relative RMS of delta(-ln T) on object rays: with XCIST's own spectrum, detector weights and attenuation, CTsynth's forward model reproduces XCIST to 0.47% (G3 120 kVp) to 1.05% (G1 80 kVp); this is an adapter/geometry check (shared transport equation), not independent physics validation. Replacing XCIST's spectrum by our spectra_v1 raises the mismatch to 2.09% (G3 120 kVp) to 2.58% (G2 80 kVp) with a systematic positive bias (our spectra attenuate more), the largest single contribution; attenuation-table choice (xraylib vs Geant4) changes projections by ~0.1-0.2% and the ideal-vs-XCIST detector response by <=~1% (sign depends on kVp). Edge rays carry the largest errors (max 0.4-0.8 in -ln units) so RMS is edge-dominated.

## Limitations

Simulated reference is itself a model. 2D 1-row geometry; disks only; no scatter, no photon counting, no reconstruction-domain comparison. Spectra are different specifications, not a validated 'truth'. 20-bin XCIST quadrature shared by all stages. Preliminary until independently reviewed.
