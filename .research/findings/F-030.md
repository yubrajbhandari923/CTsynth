# F-030 · SECTION B.6 ANSWERED on REAL kit data (3 cases): classical_simplex's apparent win is an ARTIFACT OF CLIPPING, not a benefit of the simplex constraint. Simplex projection gives RMSE 0.3787; naively clipping the same sum_to_one solution to [0,1] gives 0.3789 - identical to 4 digits. So the projection contributes essentially nothing beyond bounding the outliers it was given. Diagnostic: 43.9% of sum_to_one pixels fall OUTSIDE the physical range [0,1] and the largest magnitude is 23.6 where the valid range is [0,1], i.e. the underlying estimate is not merely noisy but grossly unphysical (consequence of the 6-8% linear-model mismatch amplified by sigma_max=343, F-025). The honest ranking on real data is: unconstrained 0.3173 ~ unconstrained+clip 0.3172 < simplex 0.3787 ~ sum_to_one+clip 0.3789 < zero_prediction 0.4465 << sum_to_one 1.5554. The UNCONSTRAINED estimator is best, and imposing sum-to-one actively harms, because the identifying constraint is applied where the model that justifies it does not hold.

## Statement

SECTION B.6 ANSWERED on REAL kit data (3 cases): classical_simplex's apparent win is an ARTIFACT OF CLIPPING, not a benefit of the simplex constraint. Simplex projection gives RMSE 0.3787; naively clipping the same sum_to_one solution to [0,1] gives 0.3789 - identical to 4 digits. So the projection contributes essentially nothing beyond bounding the outliers it was given. Diagnostic: 43.9% of sum_to_one pixels fall OUTSIDE the physical range [0,1] and the largest magnitude is 23.6 where the valid range is [0,1], i.e. the underlying estimate is not merely noisy but grossly unphysical (consequence of the 6-8% linear-model mismatch amplified by sigma_max=343, F-025). The honest ranking on real data is: unconstrained 0.3173 ~ unconstrained+clip 0.3172 < simplex 0.3787 ~ sum_to_one+clip 0.3789 < zero_prediction 0.4465 << sum_to_one 1.5554. The UNCONSTRAINED estimator is best, and imposing sum-to-one actively harms, because the identifying constraint is applied where the model that justifies it does not hold.
