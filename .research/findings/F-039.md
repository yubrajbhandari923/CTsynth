# F-039 · EXP-024 NEGATIVE RESULT (synthetic AAPM, validation, noise-free): no tested output parameterisation or soft-partition penalty is shown to improve physics-only label-free s1 over the EXP-023 baseline

## Statement

Seven arms x 3 seeds (EXP-023 recipe: 800 train/100 val, depth4 width32, 6000 steps): 3-seed mean s1 softplus 0.296, linear 0.326, occ_softmax 0.242 (seed range 0.26), 3-channel softmax 0.544, soft partition 1.6e-5 0.360, 1.6e-4 0.484, oracle-S softmax 0.424; zero prediction 0.446. The registered rule (mean below baseline by >2x pooled seed range) is met by no arm (reviewer recomputed under three readings of 'pooled range'). The rule had no stated detection floor: with occ_softmax's range of 0.26 it could only trigger below mean s1 ~0.02, so the result is 'none shown to improve', not 'none improves'. Projection RMSE does not rank s1 across arms (5.5e-4..1.2e-3 spans s1 0.09..0.36); within occ_softmax the three seeds rank the same way on both. The soft-partition arms did NOT change the sum materially (tissue |S-1| 0.035-0.080, vs 0.034-0.051 for the softplus baseline), and softmax3/oracle are confounded by saturation, so whether pinning the sum helps is UNTESTED.
