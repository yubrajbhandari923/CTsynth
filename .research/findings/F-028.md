# F-028 · CORRECTS F-017 (reviewer3). F-017 said the whole-tree git_dirty flag is 'ALWAYS true' during a research run exec run. That is too strong and is contradicted by our own record: RUN-0006's run_meta.json has git_dirty=false. The accurate statement is that git_dirty is true whenever Research OS's own writes land in the tracked tree BEFORE runmeta.collect() samples git status, which depends on write timing within the run, not on a guarantee. The remedies stand and are what actually fixed it: experiment outputs were untracked (projects/*/results*/ gitignored) and runmeta now records git_dirty_source (src/tests/configs only), which is the correct citability signal regardless of Research OS bookkeeping.
