# F-031 · SECTION B.7 VERIFIED: our s1 implementation matches the OFFICIAL AAPM kit formula exactly (0.405630414570 vs 0.405630414570, rtol 1e-12) on a random 4-case 3-material 16x16 test, including the kit's (nmat, nim, nx, ny) axis order which differs from our (nim, nmat, nx, ny) layout - the aggregation (sum over materials and pixels divided by nmat*nx*ny per case, then mean over cases) is order-independent and we reproduce it. Divisor on the real task is 3*512^2 = 786432 per case, so reduced-resolution s1 values are NOT comparable to published numbers. s2 was separately verified against the kit loop earlier, including its range(nx-roisize) quirk that omits the last ROI position.
