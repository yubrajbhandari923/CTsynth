# F-005 · SUPERSEDES F-004. Spectral mismatch always produces genuine bias; the apparent 'no bias' cells were a detection-power and scope artifact of the pre-registered criterion. Noise-free calculation (MLE on EXACT expected counts from perturbed spectra, zero Poisson noise, 20cm water + 0.2 g/cm2 iodine): every condition gives nonzero bias. Relative to the 20-realisation detection floor (CRLB/sqrt(20)) at 1e6 photons: dual-kVp iodine tilt +-10% = 0.93-1.05x (genuinely at the floor: UNDER-POWERED, not unbiased), dual-kVp iodine energy-scale +-2% = 4.0-4.2x (CLEARLY BIASED: the original F-004 wording was wrong for this case), dual-kVp WATER tilt = 3.8-4.1x (the iodine-only criterion hid a large water bias), 4-bin photon counting = 9-21x on the 2-material phantom. Two defects in the pre-registered criterion: it scored iodine only, and it required 3x the floor at BOTH dose levels so the noisier 1e5 level dominated the verdict.

## Statement

SUPERSEDES F-004. Spectral mismatch always produces genuine bias; the apparent 'no bias' cells were a detection-power and scope artifact of the pre-registered criterion. Noise-free calculation (MLE on EXACT expected counts from perturbed spectra, zero Poisson noise, 20cm water + 0.2 g/cm2 iodine): every condition gives nonzero bias. Relative to the 20-realisation detection floor (CRLB/sqrt(20)) at 1e6 photons: dual-kVp iodine tilt +-10% = 0.93-1.05x (genuinely at the floor: UNDER-POWERED, not unbiased), dual-kVp iodine energy-scale +-2% = 4.0-4.2x (CLEARLY BIASED: the original F-004 wording was wrong for this case), dual-kVp WATER tilt = 3.8-4.1x (the iodine-only criterion hid a large water bias), 4-bin photon counting = 9-21x on the 2-material phantom. Two defects in the pre-registered criterion: it scored iodine only, and it required 3x the floor at BOTH dose levels so the noisier 1e5 level dominated the verdict.
