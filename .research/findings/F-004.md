# F-004 · PARTIAL FALSIFICATION of pre-registered prediction (f): a nominal-spectrum estimator on mismatched-spectrum data is NOT detectably biased in every design. 7 of 12 cells passed. All four dual-kVp cells on the 2-material phantom failed (bias only 0.73-1.77x its noise floor), as did one 4-bin cell. Photon-counting designs showed clear mismatch bias (6-8x floor) while dual-kVp did not. Interpretation (preliminary, needs a dedicated experiment): at +-10% tilt and +-2% energy scale the dual-kVp mismatch bias is at or below the detection floor of 20 realisations, so (f) was under-powered rather than physically wrong; an alternative explanation is that 2-material dual-kVp genuinely absorbs smooth spectral error into the basis coefficients. These are NOT distinguished by this experiment.

## Statement

PARTIAL FALSIFICATION of pre-registered prediction (f): a nominal-spectrum estimator on mismatched-spectrum data is NOT detectably biased in every design. 7 of 12 cells passed. All four dual-kVp cells on the 2-material phantom failed (bias only 0.73-1.77x its noise floor), as did one 4-bin cell. Photon-counting designs showed clear mismatch bias (6-8x floor) while dual-kVp did not. Interpretation (preliminary, needs a dedicated experiment): at +-10% tilt and +-2% energy scale the dual-kVp mismatch bias is at or below the detection floor of 20 realisations, so (f) was under-powered rather than physically wrong; an alternative explanation is that 2-material dual-kVp genuinely absorbs smooth spectral error into the basis coefficients. These are NOT distinguished by this experiment.
