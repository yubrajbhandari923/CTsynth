# F-041 · Exact F-012 null direction is ~330x less visible to the projection loss than the pure A<->F swap; n = swap + calcification (-0.78%) + tissue-sum change (-3.1%) per unit

## Statement

RUN-0091 (profile recomputed with the exact float64 null vector n=(-0.72264,0.69118,-0.00782), M@n~1e-15; the first profile in RUN-0086 used the 3-decimal vector and is withdrawn). 12 val cases, mean-over-rays projection RMSE: truth+t*n rises linearly at ~4e-5 per unit t (4.0e-6 at 0.1, 1.6e-5 at 0.4); truth+t*swap, swap=(-1,1,0)/sqrt2 with calcification fixed, rises ~1.3e-2 per unit (ratio ~330). cos(n,swap)=0.9997, but n - 0.9997*swap = (-0.0157,-0.0157,-0.0078) per unit t: n contains a calcification change of -0.78% AND a total adipose+fibro change of -3.1% (sum -0.039). Compensation by calcification alone would leave ~5% of the swap's visibility, by the sum alone ~12% (delta review): both are needed. null_dir_error_frac ~0.99+ is uninformative because cos(n,swap)=0.9997 (a 'null coordinate' is effectively a swap coordinate). Trained solutions have tissue calcification 0-1.8e-3 and |S-1| ~0.03-0.08 (EXP-024), and their projection RMSE (5.5e-4..1.3e-2) is 4-6 orders above the 1.3e-8 truth floor: their errors are visible to the data, not hidden along n. HOW the trained networks reach projection RMSE ~1e-3 with adipose errors of |t|~0.1-1 (errors that vary spatially and partly cancel along rays? loss weighted toward high-signal rays?) is NOT explained by this finding. Whether the truth is the unique minimiser along the null family is not shown.

## Limitations

12 val cases; mean-over-rays RMSE; zero-thickness null vector (F-025); linear-in-t RMSE is first-order beam hardening; a uniform swap profile cannot be compared with spatially varying network errors; delta review (T-071) verdict needs_work, wording corrected here; not re-reviewed.
