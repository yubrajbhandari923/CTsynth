# F-042 · Softmax-output runs (3-channel, oracle-S) end as dead-gradient one-hot states in most seeds; cause not isolated; converged-oracle identifiability untested

## Statement

MECHANISM (dead-gradient end state), not an isolated cause: 3-channel softmax and oracle-S softmax runs end as constant one-hot (RUN-0083 all fibro, RUN-0085 all adipose, RUN-0067/68 constant; RUN-0084 a 75:25 spatial mix uncorrelated with truth) with softmax max prob 1.0 on 100% of tissue pixels. Gradient norm is exactly 0 in RUN-0083/85/67/68 and <1e-10 (not 0) in RUN-0084 and RUN-0088, below 1e-8 from step ~96-133 (RUN-0088: ~261). Lowering lr 10x (RUN-0088) only delayed the collapse and gave an identical end state; training loss falls ~50x during it, so this is descent into a simplex vertex, not overshoot. With the 1e-8 threshold RUN-0089/90 keep live gradients, but RUN-0090 has grad norm <1e-6 in 57% of steps; they reach s1 0.309/0.323 (no better than baseline) at projection RMSE 4-6e-3 (under-trained). Nothing here shows that sum knowledge identifies the split, or that it does not. Untested candidate driver: the uniform softmax start puts 1/3 calcification in every pixel, which the data penalise strongly.

## Limitations

Cause not isolated; 'saturation causes the collapse' is close to circular; one intervention (lr); 3 seeds; review R1 needs_work, corrected.
