# F-011 · AMENDS F-009 (two numerical/scope corrections from the 2nd reviewer's verdict check, verified by Opus). (1) The 4-bin photon-counting bias on P2 is 27-43x the floor over all 8 (condition x material) cells, NOT the 21-38x stated in F-009. (2) The dual-kVp iodine TILT case is MARGINAL and must not be quoted as strongly detectable: per-ray bias is only 0.66-0.75 of the floor and the signed-mean z of about 5 arises only by pooling 48 rays. (3) All z values are noise-free hypothetical power computed against a noise-only null, i.e. the bias an infinite-data experiment would see relative to a 20-realisation floor - they are NOT observed test statistics from the Monte-Carlo run. F-009's central claim (mismatch always biases; the rms criterion is defective; dual-kVp water tilt bias is large and was hidden by iodine-only scoring) is unaffected.

## Statement

AMENDS F-009 (two numerical/scope corrections from the 2nd reviewer's verdict check, verified by Opus). (1) The 4-bin photon-counting bias on P2 is 27-43x the floor over all 8 (condition x material) cells, NOT the 21-38x stated in F-009. (2) The dual-kVp iodine TILT case is MARGINAL and must not be quoted as strongly detectable: per-ray bias is only 0.66-0.75 of the floor and the signed-mean z of about 5 arises only by pooling 48 rays. (3) All z values are noise-free hypothetical power computed against a noise-only null, i.e. the bias an infinite-data experiment would see relative to a 20-realisation floor - they are NOT observed test statistics from the Monte-Carlo run. F-009's central claim (mismatch always biases; the rms criterion is defective; dual-kVp water tilt bias is large and was hidden by iodine-only scoring) is unaffected.
