## Statement

HELLWEGE 2026 IS NOT A PRECEDENT FOR OUR PROBLEM (full arXiv PDF text read). Their task is 2 basis materials (soft tissue, calcification) from 2 spectra: SQUARE and determined by counting, so pixelwise identifiability is automatic and never discussed in the paper. Our AAPM task is 3 materials from 2 spectra: underdetermined, and in their Eq. 6 objective L = (1/|D|)(1/M) sum_i [||p_LE - phat_LE||^2 + ||p_HE - phat_HE||^2] (unweighted MSE on log line integrals, no I0, no Poisson term, NO regulariser, no nonnegativity, no sum-to-one, no TV, no entropy, no softmax) our F-014 null direction would be EXACTLY FLAT. The paper reports no ablations at all; its gain over MBIR (VMI RMSE 5.83 vs 13.07 HU at 50 keV) comes from the network and training, not from the loss. The authors state the inverse crime explicitly (Sec 4.2: same polychromatic model generates and decodes) and run no mismatch experiment. Early stopping uses projection-domain validation loss with no ground truth, which is a genuinely label-free protocol worth copying. Unverified: energy-bin count, final activation, how 3+ AAPM tissue classes were merged into 2, whether data were noisy, and Table 1 mean-vs-median.
