# F-008 · AMENDS F-003 (scope). The dose-independent misspecification bias is an IODINE LINE-INTEGRAL statistic in g/cm2 (not g/cm3 density), measured on 27 rays of the 3-material phantom with a 2-material estimator. Both designs agree (bias ratio 1e6/1e5: M2 0.89, M4 1.02; 7.0x and 15.0x their noise floors). Dose-independence of misspecification bias is expected from theory, so this is a confirmation that the implementation behaves correctly, not a discovery.

## Statement

AMENDS F-003 (scope). The dose-independent misspecification bias is an IODINE LINE-INTEGRAL statistic in g/cm2 (not g/cm3 density), measured on 27 rays of the 3-material phantom with a 2-material estimator. Both designs agree (bias ratio 1e6/1e5: M2 0.89, M4 1.02; 7.0x and 15.0x their noise floors). Dose-independence of misspecification bias is expected from theory, so this is a confirmation that the implementation behaves correctly, not a discovery.
