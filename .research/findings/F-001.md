# F-001 · Material identifiability in this toy spectral CT model is set by the rank of the Poisson-weighted count Jacobian, not by the estimator: one energy-integrating measurement gives rank 1 on every sampled ray (cannot separate 2+ materials), dual-kVp gives rank 2 (separates 2 materials, median condition number 195), and dual-kVp cannot separate 3 materials (rank 2 < 3). Four photon-counting bins separate 3 materials but with 2.70x the iodine CRLB noise of dual-kVp 2-material at equal total photons.

## Statement

Material identifiability in this toy spectral CT model is set by the rank of the Poisson-weighted count Jacobian, not by the estimator: one energy-integrating measurement gives rank 1 on every sampled ray (cannot separate 2+ materials), dual-kVp gives rank 2 (separates 2 materials, median condition number 195), and dual-kVp cannot separate 3 materials (rank 2 < 3). Four photon-counting bins separate 3 materials but with 2.70x the iodine CRLB noise of dual-kVp 2-material at equal total photons.
