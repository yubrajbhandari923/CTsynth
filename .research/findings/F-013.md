# F-013 · AAPM phantom maps are a TWO-REGIME partition, not a plain simplex (verified directly on the 10 kit sample cases, case 0): the three maps sum to exactly 0 in 35.1% of pixels (air background, no fourth material class), to 1 within 4e-8 in 59.6%, and lie strictly between 0 and 1 in only 5.2% (boundary/partial-volume pixels). Maxima: adipose 1.0, fibroglandular 1.0, calcification 0.566 (10-case sample). A classical estimator must therefore handle air explicitly - imposing sum-to-one globally would be wrong for 35% of pixels - and the 5.2% transition pixels are where a hard two-class assumption will fail. Arrays verified: phantoms/images (N,512,512) float32, transmission (N,256,1024) float32 in (0,1].

## Statement

AAPM phantom maps are a TWO-REGIME partition, not a plain simplex (verified directly on the 10 kit sample cases, case 0): the three maps sum to exactly 0 in 35.1% of pixels (air background, no fourth material class), to 1 within 4e-8 in 59.6%, and lie strictly between 0 and 1 in only 5.2% (boundary/partial-volume pixels). Maxima: adipose 1.0, fibroglandular 1.0, calcification 0.566 (10-case sample). A classical estimator must therefore handle air explicitly - imposing sum-to-one globally would be wrong for 35% of pixels - and the 5.2% transition pixels are where a hard two-class assumption will fail. Arrays verified: phantoms/images (N,512,512) float32, transmission (N,256,1024) float32 in (0,1].
