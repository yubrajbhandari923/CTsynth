# F-006 · SUPERSEDES F-002. Empirical Monte-Carlo std agrees with the Cramer-Rao bound to within 12.5% (median over rays; range 0.875-1.106) for the three identifiable cells at 1e6 photons, with a systematic pattern (P2 cells low 0.87-0.95, P3 k=3 cells high 1.05-1.11) rather than scatter about 1. The sampling spread of this check is ~3-4% (median of per-ray std estimates over 26-54 rays, 20 draws each), so the deviation is real, not check noise. This is a SELF-CONSISTENCY check of Jacobian + sampler + MLE under one shared model and table: a wrong attenuation table or spectrum would pass it equally. It is not validation of physical fidelity.

This is a SELF-CONSISTENCY check of Jacobian + sampler + MLE under one shared model and table: a wrong attenuation table or spectrum would pass it equally. It is not validation of physical fidelity.
