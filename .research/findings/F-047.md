# F-047 · EXP-036 arms C (three free fractions) and A_img (image-derived support, sum=1) did not test sum-free identifiability: the image-derived support cannot hold partial-volume pixels (truth masked to the support already has data MSE 1.6e-5 vs ~1e-10 unmasked), every C init plateaus at 2.7-2.8e-6 and A_img at 4.7-4.9e-6; their reported band RMS (1e5-1e6; 1.5) are metric artefacts. Bimodality prior (arm D, lambda 1e-5) gave no gain over arm A (s1 0.087-0.100 vs 0.014-0.048).

## Statement

EXP-036 arms C (three free fractions) and A_img (image-derived support, sum=1) did not test sum-free identifiability: every C run hits max_iter at 2.7-2.8e-6 and is still falling ~1.4% per 100 it (solver-limited not excluded), the C swap/null coordinates stay at the init value (0.30-0.33), and the oracle-perturbed C inits already have data MSE 1.6-1.75e-5 (perturbation + support mask), far above the unmasked truth (1.8e-16) so a support misspecification is plausible but inferred, not isolated. C band RMS (1e5-1e6) is a ratio blow-up artefact; A_img's 1.5 mixes unclipped excursions with real error at tissue pixels outside the mask. Arm D (bimodality, lambda 1e-5, max_iter, MSE 3.4-7.2e-8, not converged) shows no material gain over A (unclipped s1 and hf marginally better).

## Limitations

support-misspecification not tested by a support-oracle run; one lambda for D; unreviewed beyond the targeted pass
