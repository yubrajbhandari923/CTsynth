# F-040 · RUN-0066 (occ_softmax seed 2, s1 0.0914) is a genuine structured label-free solution; why it arose is unresolved

## Statement

RUN-0066 (occ_softmax seed 2) has s1 0.0914 (100 val cases; per-case 0.079-0.106, all <0.15; tissue adipose Pearson 0.970 with truth, bimodal histogram like truth, 83% of tissue pixels within 0.1). The per-case formula was recomputed independently; the aggregate reuses s1_mean_rmse, so the metric definition itself is not independently checked. It is the only structured solution of 21 physics-only runs; occ_softmax seeds 0 and 1 gave s1 0.351 (uniform collapse) and 0.282 (partial). Its 100-case projection RMSE is 1.11e-3, the lowest of the three occ_softmax seeds (1.11e-3 / 1.75e-3 / 7.7e-3 rank the seeds identically to s1), so WITHIN this arm the data term does track s1; ACROSS arms it does not (softplus seeds reach 5.5e-4 with s1 ~0.3). Whether the projection loss 'selected' this solution is therefore unresolved. Mechanism unknown; success probability not estimable from 3 seeds.

## Limitations

Single run, validation only, label-free checkpoint selection (best val projection loss); mechanism hypotheses untested; 12-case diag value 1.2e-3 differs from the 100-case 1.11e-3; review R1 needs_work, corrected.
