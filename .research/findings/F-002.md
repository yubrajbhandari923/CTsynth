## Statement

The forward model plus per-ray Poisson MLE is statistically well-calibrated: empirical Monte-Carlo standard deviation of estimated line integrals matches the Cramer-Rao bound within 11% for every identifiable cell at 1e6 photons (0.87-1.11 across water/iodine/calcium), with zero LM convergence failures. This validates the forward model, Jacobian and estimator as a consistent stack.
