# F-010 · SUPERSEDES F-007 (2nd independent review). Identifiability: STRUCTURAL part (rank <= min(n_meas,k), so 1 measurement cannot separate 2+ materials and dual-kVp cannot separate 3) is arithmetic, not evidence, and holds at any threshold. EMPIRICAL part, on the real ray subset at 1e6 photons: rank verdicts are robust for rank thresholds 1e-3 to 1e-10 (100% full rank) but NOT at 1e-2, where the full-rank fraction collapses to 0% for dual-kVp k=2 and 4-bin k=3 and 58% for 4-bin k=2, because smallest/largest singular-value ratios are only 0.0020-0.0123. (The superseded F-007 wrongly claimed robustness up to 1e-2, contradicting the ratios quoted in its own sentence.) Median condition numbers: dual-kVp k=2 195 (max 506), 4-bin k=2 99, 4-bin k=3 217. At EQUAL basis size k=2 and equal total unattenuated photons, 4-bin photon counting has median iodine CRLB 0.00151 vs dual-kVp 0.00376 g/cm^2 = 2.5x better by median (2.4x by rms aggregation; the ratio is ray-dependent). This compares an idealised energy-weighted 2-exposure design against an idealised 4-window 1-exposure design with NO detector response, charge sharing or pile-up, so it is an upper bound on the photon-counting advantage.

## Statement

SUPERSEDES F-007 (2nd independent review). Identifiability: STRUCTURAL part (rank <= min(n_meas,k), so 1 measurement cannot separate 2+ materials and dual-kVp cannot separate 3) is arithmetic, not evidence, and holds at any threshold. EMPIRICAL part, on the real ray subset at 1e6 photons: rank verdicts are robust for rank thresholds 1e-3 to 1e-10 (100% full rank) but NOT at 1e-2, where the full-rank fraction collapses to 0% for dual-kVp k=2 and 4-bin k=3 and 58% for 4-bin k=2, because smallest/largest singular-value ratios are only 0.0020-0.0123. (The superseded F-007 wrongly claimed robustness up to 1e-2, contradicting the ratios quoted in its own sentence.) Median condition numbers: dual-kVp k=2 195 (max 506), 4-bin k=2 99, 4-bin k=3 217. At EQUAL basis size k=2 and equal total unattenuated photons, 4-bin photon counting has median iodine CRLB 0.00151 vs dual-kVp 0.00376 g/cm^2 = 2.5x better by median (2.4x by rms aggregation; the ratio is ray-dependent). This compares an idealised energy-weighted 2-exposure design against an idealised 4-window 1-exposure design with NO detector response, charge sharing or pile-up, so it is an upper bound on the photon-counting advantage.
