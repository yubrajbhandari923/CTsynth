## Statement

Fitting a 2-material basis to 3-material data produces bias that does NOT shrink with photon count (bias ratio 1e6/1e5 = 0.89 and 1.02 versus 0.32 expected for noise-driven error), at 7-15x the noise floor: basis misspecification is a systematic error that more dose cannot fix.
