# F-022 · EXP-004 CAPACITY CONTROL (Opus, post-hoc but decisive): the learned rungs' ~0.5 tissue-RMSE plateau is NOT an optimisation artifact. Training the SUPERVISED rung (labels, hence no identifiability problem) far beyond the EXP-004 budget saturates: 80ep/w8/d1 = 0.0642, 400ep/w8/d1 = 0.0385, 400ep/w16/d2 = 0.0391, 1500ep/w16/d2 = 0.0372 (19x epochs and 4x capacity buy 0.064->0.037). The error therefore decomposes cleanly: the PROBLEM is exactly solvable (classical sum_to_one tissue RMSE 1e-6), the NETWORK/representation costs 0.037 even with labels, and the LABEL-FREE penalty is 0.439 = 92% of B3's total error. The identifiability gap dominates the network gap by ~12x, so P1 (low consistency loss + large material error) is confirmed and is not explained by undertraining.

## Statement

EXP-004 CAPACITY CONTROL (Opus, post-hoc but decisive): the learned rungs' ~0.5 tissue-RMSE plateau is NOT an optimisation artifact. Training the SUPERVISED rung (labels, hence no identifiability problem) far beyond the EXP-004 budget saturates: 80ep/w8/d1 = 0.0642, 400ep/w8/d1 = 0.0385, 400ep/w16/d2 = 0.0391, 1500ep/w16/d2 = 0.0372 (19x epochs and 4x capacity buy 0.064->0.037). The error therefore decomposes cleanly: the PROBLEM is exactly solvable (classical sum_to_one tissue RMSE 1e-6), the NETWORK/representation costs 0.037 even with labels, and the LABEL-FREE penalty is 0.439 = 92% of B3's total error. The identifiability gap dominates the network gap by ~12x, so P1 (low consistency loss + large material error) is confirmed and is not explained by undertraining.
