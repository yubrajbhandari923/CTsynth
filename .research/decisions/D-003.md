# D-003 · D-G pre-registration and review standards

## Decision

Pre-registered success criteria must score all materials, state a detection floor from a power analysis computed before running, avoid requiring significance at every condition, separate structural from empirical results, and avoid confounding two factors. Superseding findings require their own independent review.

## Reason

EXP-002 review found three overstatements in Opus's own findings: arithmetic reported as evidence, a self-consistency check called validation, and a 'no bias' claim that a noise-free calculation disproved. The defective criterion was iodine-only and dominated by the noisier dose level.
