# D-004 · D-H bias detection statistic

## Decision

The correct test for a systematic bias measured over many rays is the SIGNED MEAN over rays compared to floor/sqrt(n_rays), not an rms compared to an unsubtracted noise floor. An rms statistic is inflated by noise and cannot distinguish 'no bias' from 'bias below the rms detection limit'; it declared a bias with z=5 to be 'at the noise floor'.

## Reason

EXP-002 second review: dual-kVp iodine tilt bias had rms/floor 0.82-0.92 (apparently null) but signed-mean z = +4.6/-5.2 (clearly real).
