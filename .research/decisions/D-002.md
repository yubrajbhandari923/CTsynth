# D-002 · D-F iodine K-edge scope limit for EXP-002

## Decision

EXP-002 runs on the 1 keV table as-is; iodine K-edge (33.17 keV) is under-resolved and the shared forward/estimator table makes this a shared-model limitation, not a forward/estimator mismatch. Iodine ABSOLUTE accuracy claims are therefore out of scope for EXP-002; identifiability/conditioning/noise-amplification claims remain valid because they depend on the spectral contrast actually present in the model used. A K-edge-resolved table (v2, fine grid near edges) is required before any quantitative iodine-concentration claim.
