# D-011 · XCIST is the first independent CT forward-data producer; DukeSim deferred

## Decision

XCIST is the first independent CT forward-data producer; DukeSim deferred

## Reason

Human decision. XCIST 1.6.9 (BSD-3) is open, installed on laptop and Duke SLURM (EXP-009/011/012), and verified through an independent analytic reference (adapter conventions) and a staged mismatch ladder (F-037). It is a producer of measurements only: truth = input phantom; CTsynth's differentiable PyTorch model and fraction/density representation are unchanged. XCIST's published detector baseline is energy-integrating; a PC model exists (CZT response matrix of undocumented origin, no pile-up/charge-sharing) but is NOT used or validated - realistic PCCT detection is deferred (T-024). DukeSim integration waits for collaborator data (docs/dukesim-request.md, T-025). XCIST internal 20-bin energy quadrature is not an independent energy-channel measurement.
