# D-005 · D-I AAPM benchmark: noiseless exact recovery + simplex-constrained 3-material inversion

## Decision

AAPM benchmark design is governed by two facts established in discovery: (1) the challenge data are NOISELESS and the forward model was shared with all participants, so this is an EXACT-RECOVERY problem (winning s1 RMSE 6.8e-7, i.e. ~1e-6 is reachable and is NOT a noise floor) - a denoising-style evaluation would be meaningless; (2) 3-material recovery from 2 spectral measurements is made well posed by a SUM-TO-ONE constraint on VOLUME FRACTIONS (2 spectral equations + 1 partition constraint = 3 equations, unique when the three (mu_low, mu_high) points are non-collinear), not by nonnegativity or priors. Therefore: the classical baseline uses volume fractions with an explicit simplex constraint; nonnegativity alone is insufficient; and our metric must match the published s1 = mean over cases of sqrt(||t-r||^2/(3n)) on dimensionless volume fractions.
