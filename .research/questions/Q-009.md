# Q-009 · P09-Q1: Can a 3D U-Net recover XCAT-derived physical material concentration maps (g/cm3 per elemental composition) from simulated monoenergetic COPD CT of unseen patients, such that the predicted phantom reproduces the input CT through LEAP and transfers to a different photon energy?

## Description

Follows EXP-039 (15-class segmentation, F-049), which says nothing about physical composition. Target: per-voxel mass concentration c_k (g/cm3) of each distinct elemental composition k, so mu(r,E)=sum_k c_k (mu/rho)_k(E) exactly equals the simulator's box-averaged mu. The 70 keV image gives ONE number per voxel; compositions are near-degenerate in attenuation shape (soft tissue/lung/skin/aorta within ~1 deg), so the inverse is non-identifiable from CT intensity alone and any success may come from anatomical priors, not physics. Scope: simulated CT of the same 67 XCAT COPD phantoms, 47/10/10 frozen split, no real data.
