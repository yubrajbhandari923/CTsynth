## Findings

- **F-012** AAPM 3-material conditioning, computed from the official starting-kit spectra and mu tables (md5-verified kit, 10 sample cases): the spectrum-weighted effective attenuation matrix is [[0.26697,0.31321,3.01182],[0.21930,0.24788,1.64238]] 1/cm for (adipose,fibroglandular,calcification) at 50/80 kVp. Adipose and fibroglandular are spectrally near-degenerate: column cosine 0.999834, i.e. 1.04 degrees apart. The unconstrained 2x3 null direction is [-0.723,+0.691,-0.008], confirming F-010's prediction concretely: adipose can be traded against fibroglandular with essentially no measurement change. Appending the sum-to-one row makes the 3x3 system full rank (singular values 3.697, 1.176, 0.00290) but POORLY CONDITIONED at cond=1273. Therefore the partition constraint restores identifiability but error in the adipose/fibroglandular direction is amplified ~1000x; exact recovery in the noiseless challenge data is possible, while any model mismatch or interpolation error is magnified by that factor. Image-domain linear inversion is expected to be far worse than the published model-based winners (6.8e-7). (preliminary)
- **F-013** AAPM phantom maps are a TWO-REGIME partition, not a plain simplex (verified directly on the 10 kit sample cases, case 0): the three maps sum to exactly 0 in 35.1% of pixels (air background, no fourth material class), to 1 within 4e-8 in 59.6%, and lie strictly between 0 and 1 in only 5.2% (boundary/partial-volume pixels). Maxima: adipose 1.0, fibroglandular 1.0, calcification 0.566 (10-case sample). A classical estimator must therefore handle air explicitly - imposing sum-to-one globally would be wrong for 35% of pixels - and the 5.2% transition pixels are where a hard two-class assumption will fail. Arrays verified: phantoms/images (N,512,512) float32, transmission (N,256,1024) float32 in (0,1]. (preliminary)
- **F-014** F-012's null space is now demonstrated OPERATIONALLY, not just algebraically (synthetic data, src/ctsynth/aapm/classical.py + tests/aapm/test_classical.py). Unconstrained per-pixel inversion of the 2x3 AAPM system genuinely fails (max per-pixel error > 0.05 in volume fraction) and its error vector is parallel to the SVD null direction to <1e-6 deg; that independently-computed null vector lies 0.024 deg from the published F-012 vector [-0.723,+0.691,-0.008]. Crucially the error is INVISIBLE to the data: |e @ mu^T| < 1e-12, i.e. the wrong answer reproduces the measurements exactly. Adding the sum-to-one row restores a unique solution (exact recovery to ~1e-10 in float64 on noiseless synthetic tissue pixels) but amplifies measurement perturbation: the derived worst-direction amplification sigma_max(B^-1[:,:2])*|d|/|x| = 181 for a typical 50/50 adipose/fibroglandular pixel, matched to rel 1e-4, which is 0.14x cond(B)=1273 (below cond because the appended sum row is exact and unperturbed). (preliminary)
- **F-015** REFINES F-013 (boundary pixels are the hard case, not merely a partial-volume nuisance). In the two-regime AAPM partition, pixels whose three fractions sum strictly between 0 and 1 (~5% of the image) are mis-modelled by a sum-to-one estimator: the unit-sum row CONTRADICTS the measurement there, and that contradiction is amplified by the ~1273 conditioning, so the resulting error is far LARGER than the sum deficit (1 - sum) itself. Simplex projection only clips the result and does not fix the model error. Consequence for the benchmark: boundary-band error must be reported separately (it is, in the evaluation record), and an air/tissue decision rule is a MODELLING CHOICE, not a fact recoverable from the data - the implementation uses AIR_CUTOFF_PER_CM = 0.1 (half the smallest tissue mu from F-012), which is untested on real AAPM data. (preliminary)
