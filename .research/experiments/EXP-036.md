# EXP-036 · Stage 1: converged per-case reduced-parameter solves (information bound, optimisation removed from networks)

## Hypothesis

H1 (information): given the exact polychromatic forward model, the tissue support from the images (air cutoff 0.1/cm, no labels) and calcification absence outside a sparse support, the A/F split is recovered label-free by a per-case solve to s1 <= 0.05 from an uninformative start. H1' (optimisation): the EXP-023/032 failures reflect conditioning and starting point, not missing spectral information. Alternatives falsified if the information-rich arm does NOT recover.

## Motivation


## Method

Per-case Adam/L-BFGS on 20 frozen VALIDATION cases (arms fixed now; no tuning on labels; stop by projection loss plateau, max 3000 iterations, 32 views/step then full-view polish). Arms: (A) support known from images, sum=1 in tissue, calcification=0, unknown a(x) in [0,1] (1 DOF/pixel); (B) support known, sum=1, calcification sparse L1 free (lambda from Stage 0 window); (C) physics only, F>=0, no sum/calc constraint, converged from uniform 0.5 init (EXP-032 converged from a different start); (D) A plus bimodality prior (P2) from Stage 0. Inits per arm: uniform 0.5, truth+0.3*swap, truth+0.3*n, random. Report per case and arm: s1, s1_swapped, null-coordinate and swap-coordinate error, tissue |S-1|, calcification mass outside truth support, full-view projection RMSE vs truth floor 1.3e-8, iterations to plateau, init sensitivity.

## Expected outcome


## Success criteria

Decision rules fixed now: information is sufficient if arm A reaches mean s1 <= 0.05 from the uniform init on >=18/20 cases; the contribution of calcification freedom is A vs B vs C (paired per-case differences); init sensitivity: |s1(uniform)-s1(truth+0.3 n)| > 0.05 means the minimiser is not reached from the uninformative start (optimisation-limited), <= 0.05 means information-limited. Detection floor: n-direction needs RMSE <= 3e-6 to resolve 0.1, swap needs 6e-4: report the achieved RMSE per case so unresolved cases are labelled as such, not as failures of the prior.

## Stop conditions

Test or train labels used in any arm; arm A fails the success rule => do NOT start Stage 2, report conditioning-limited; more than 3 nonfinite runs.

## Known limitations
