# EXP-039 · Inverse U-Net on simulated COPD CT: fixed vs noisy vs varied vs acquisition-conditioned training (arms A-D)

## Hypothesis

Training on varied acquisition/noise/kernel settings (C) and conditioning on acquisition metadata (D) improve organ-group segmentation of unseen patients' simulated CT relative to fixed-setting training (A, B), especially under noise and held-out acquisition settings (80 keV, pitch 1.4).

## Synthesis S1 — 2026-10-29 (agent: claude-code)

- **What happened:** Staged simulate-then-train on 67 COPD XCAT phantoms (47/10/10 patient split, 22728 and 21535 forced into train). 291 simulated CT volumes (3 variants for train/val patients, 12 for the 10 test patients) generated on 8 GPUs in 13.2 min; 8 U-Nets (arms A clean, B noisy, C varied, D varied+FiLM conditioned; 2 seeds; 40000 steps) trained in 4.76 h on 8 GPUs; one pre-registered test evaluation (run twice because my script omitted per-patient output; the pooled results reproduced exactly). Macro foreground Dice on unseen test patients, clean CT: A 0.917, B/C/D 0.911; intensity baseline 0.271. Noise N0=1e5: A 0.691, B 0.908, C 0.905, D 0.904. Smoother kernel (LEAP low-pass W=4): A 0.670, B 0.716, C 0.892, D 0.893. Held-out 80 keV: A 0.736, B 0.800, C 0.840, D 0.848. Held-out pitch 1.4: A 0.802, B 0.911, C 0.908, D 0.907. Per-class: 12/14 classes Dice >= 0.89 on clean CT; 'lung' 0.37-0.40 and fissure 0.75.
- **What worked:** Noise-aware training removes the collapse of clean-only training under noise (B-A +0.217 [0.156,0.276] at N0=1e5, 10/10 patients); varied training (C) generalises to unseen kernels/energy better than noisy-only (C-B +0.177 [0.170,0.184] for W=4, +0.040 [0.032,0.047] at 80 keV) at a cost <= 0.004 elsewhere; all U-Net arms exceed the intensity baseline by >= 0.62 Dice.
- **What did not work:** 80 keV (held out) degrades every arm (-0.06 to -0.18), mainly bone classes (sternum about 0.55-0.59 for all arms). The 'lung' class is not separable by CT intensity from some lung_parenchyma bins (same material). Evaluation script omitted per-patient confusion matrices on the first test run (code defect, fixed, identical results). Clean-only arm A is fragile to noise and kernel.
- **Interpretation:** Acquisition conditioning (D vs C): in-distribution -0.0007 [-0.0015,0.0000]; held-out +0.0037 [0.0022,0.0055], driven by 80 keV +0.0084 [0.0051,0.0121]; below the pre-registered floor (0.02-0.03) and inside the seed spread (C 0.829-0.850, D 0.842-0.854): NOT interpreted as an effect. Evidence supports training on varied settings, not metadata conditioning, in this simulator.
- **Limitations:** Simulated CT of the same phantoms that provide the labels (no real data, no label noise); 10 test patients (bootstrap CIs reflect patient sampling only), 2 seeds; monoenergetic, no scatter/electronic noise; N0 is photons per ray per view, not dose; borrowed Duke1 geometry; 0.25 mm native voxel size assumed; orientation provisional; only two held-out acquisition settings (80 keV, pitch 1.4); no test-set tuning; test set evaluated twice with identical outputs.
- **Unresolved:** Whether more variants per patient help (not measured); whether conditioning helps for larger acquisition shifts or with a metadata-informative loss; physical material composition recovery (not addressed); real-data transfer.
- **Recommended next:** User review. Candidate follow-ups: a third energy and wider held-out acquisition range, 5 variants/patient ablation, more seeds, real/segmentation-noise robustness.
