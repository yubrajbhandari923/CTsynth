- **What worked:** Driver, metrics at official 512x512 conventions, validation-only selection; per-case values stored.
- **What did not work:** The TV weight selection saturated at the largest grid value (0.1), so the optimum may lie beyond the grid; I did not extend the grid after seeing results on validation inside this experiment.
- **Interpretation:** All image-domain classical methods are poor on the real AAPM images: best (TV) s1 0.271 is only 1.6x better than predicting nothing (0.446), against supervised U-Net s1 0.016 (EXP-022 seed 2) and the published winners ~1e-6 scale. Pre-registered expectations hold on 100 cases: raw sum_to_one is 3.4x WORSE than the zero prediction (cond~1273 amplifies the reconstruction/beam-hardening model mismatch; consistent with F-025/F-027), and simplex projection (0.381) is worse than the raw unconstrained pinv (0.315). Constraints do not help merely because they are physical: simplex raises boundary error (0.48 vs 0.26 unconstrained). TV lowers interior RMSE (0.33 vs 0.40) but raises boundary RMSE (0.41 vs 0.26) and s2 (0.66 vs 0.43). Calcification is near-trivially small (RMSE 0.004-0.014).
- **Limitations:** Validation only; image-domain linear model ignores beam hardening (same-image approach as F-025); grid edge saturation; single air-cutoff value; no nonlinear projection-domain method here (planned as a separate experiment); statements about ranking among close methods (nonneg 0.307 vs unconstrained 0.315) are not tested for significance (per-case values saved).
- **Unresolved:** TV lambda beyond 0.1; paired per-case differences.
- **Recommended next:** Projection-domain iterative baseline (new experiment); final test evaluation of all methods at the end of the benchmark.
