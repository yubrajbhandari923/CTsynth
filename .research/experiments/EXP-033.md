# EXP-033 · EXP-024 anomaly diagnostics: occ_softmax seed 2 and oracle collapse

## Hypothesis

H-A: occ_softmax seed 2 (s1 0.091) is a real, label-free solution that the polychromatic projection loss prefers (data term lower than neighbouring null-direction shifts), not an evaluation artifact. H-B: the oracle (softmax x true S) collapse is optimisation failure by softmax saturation (grad norm exactly 0 after step ~100-133 in 98% of steps, seen in RUN-0083/84/85 and softmax3 RUN-0067/68), not insufficient identifiability: with the sum known the problem has a unique solution (F-014), so a non-saturated oracle should reach s1 well below 0.296.
