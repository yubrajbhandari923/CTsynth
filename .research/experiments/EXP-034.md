# EXP-034 · Acquisition identifiability theory: SECT vs DECT vs PCCT Jacobian rank, singular values, Fisher information

## Hypothesis

H-T: the adipose/fibroglandular null direction is structural to ANY count-equivalent attenuation measurement with near-parallel mu curves: the 50/80 kVp DECT Jacobian has rank 2 in 3 unknowns (exact null direction, F-012); partition-constrained identifiability needs the support/sum, and then the Cramer-Rao std of the adipose fraction is large (~0.25 per ray at 2e5 photons); ideal photon-counting energy binning (B bins) raises the unconstrained rank to 3 but only gains a bounded factor (< ~3x std) because mu_a(E) and mu_f(E) are nearly parallel (spectrum-weighted angle ~2.5 deg). Therefore additional spectral measurements cannot replace priors.

## Method

Deterministic noise-free calculation (projects/p05_aapm/identifiability_theory.py) on the committed kit tables: per-ray Jacobian of -log T wrt (Pa,Pf,Pc), with and without partition constraint Pa+Pf+Pc=L; SVD, Fisher information with Poisson var(-log T)=1/(N T), CRB of the sum-preserving adipose<->fibro coordinate, for SECT-80, SECT-50, DECT(50+80, photons split), PCCT-B (B=2,4,8,32 equal-photon bins of the 80 kVp beam, ideal detector) at L=2,6,12 cm and three compositions. Equal total photons (2e5/ray). No data, no training.

## Synthesis S1 — 2026-10-15 (agent: claude-code)

- **What happened:** Deterministic noise-free analysis on the committed kit tables (RUN-0087, projects/p05_aapm/identifiability_theory.py; per ray, N=2e5 photons total, ideal detector). (STRUCTURAL, forced by arithmetic): 3-unknown Jacobian rank is 1 for SECT, 2 for the 50/80 kVp DECT (min Fisher eigenvalue ~1e-11 vs max ~1e5, i.e. an exact null direction, F-012), 3 for PCCT with >=4 bins. (EMPIRICAL, from the tables): with the partition constraint Pa+Pf+Pc=L the DECT reduced Jacobian has singular values (3.46, 3.06e-3) at L=6 cm 50/50 (cond ~1.1e3, 2 unknowns, so identifiable noise-free). CRB std of the adipose fraction per ray (sum-preserving adipose<->fibro swap, nuisance unknown) at L=6 cm mixed: SECT inf; DECT 0.249; PCCT-2 0.154; PCCT-4 0.131; PCCT-8 0.123; PCCT-32 0.120. Adipose vs fibroglandular mu curves are 2.45 deg (50 kVp) / 2.67 deg (80 kVp) apart (spectrum-weighted); mu_f/mu_a ranges 1.08-1.38 across energy.
- **What worked:** Quantifies why more spectral bins help only boundedly: PCCT-32 improves the per-ray CRB over DECT by ~2.1x (4.3x in Fisher information), saturating by ~8 bins; the unconstrained smallest Fisher eigenvalue becomes nonzero (1e-5..6e-3) but remains ~1e-8 of the largest.
- **What did not work:** Not a failure; first draft had two bugs (known-nuisance CRB instead of profile CRB; photon split applied to PCCT bins) found by checking that splitting a spectrum cannot reduce Fisher information; corrected before the registered run.
- **Interpretation:** Under the ideal-bin, no-K-edge model, extra spectral measurements do not remove the adipose/fibroglandular ambiguity: DECT already identifies it noise-free ONLY when the support/sum (partition) is known, and the achievable precision is bounded by the near-parallel mu curves, improving at most ~2x in std with ideal PCCT. So priors (or the partition knowledge) remain necessary at realistic noise; the real lever is where information can come from (anatomy/statistics) not more energy bins. Mathematical/deterministic result, not yet independently reviewed.
- **Limitations:** Single-ray local analysis (no tomographic coupling or neighbourhood regularity); Poisson Gaussian-approximated; ideal photon-counting (no spectral distortion/charge sharing); 3 compositions, 3 path lengths; mu tables from the kit only; no calcification-rich rays.
- **Unresolved:** Tomographic (image-level) Fisher information with a smoothness/anatomy prior; realistic PCCT response.
- **Recommended next:** Use as the identifiability baseline for the prior-design plan; do not run PCCT experiments yet
