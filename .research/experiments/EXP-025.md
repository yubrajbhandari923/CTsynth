# EXP-025 · Physics plus prior ablations

## Hypothesis

Individual priors differ in what they select among null-direction solutions

## Motivation


## Method

projects/p05_aapm/milestone_a.py --arm physics with EXP-023's data/recipe/steps (softplus U-Net, 800/100, lr 3e-4 warmup 300 clip 1, 6000 steps) plus ONE added prior at a time via --prior NAME=W: tv (isotropic TV), entropy (per-pixel Shannon), range_hinge (relu(f-1), plausible-range), calc_l1 (calcification L1, limited-support proxy). Hard-partition (softmax), soft-partition and occupancy variants are the EXP-024 arms and are not rerun here. Scale inspection done before registering (EXP-023 seed-0 solution, 20 val cases, projection loss 9.6e-7): prior values tv 0.0130 (truth 0.0145), entropy 0.446 (truth 0.035), range_hinge 2.1e-4, calc_l1 3.5e-5; rule: w1 = 0.1*L_proj/value = tv 7.4e-6, entropy 2.2e-7, range_hinge 4.6e-4, calc_l1 2.7e-3; w2 = 10*w1. Two-stage protocol fixed now: STAGE 1 screening, 1 seed (0), both weights, 4 priors = 8 runs; STAGE 2: any prior whose stage-1 s1 differs from the EXP-023 baseline mean (0.2956) by more than 2x the EXP-023 seed range (2x0.0165) gets 3 seeds at the better weight, and a >=3-seed comparison replaces the screening claim. Pre-registered predictions (from the scale inspection, to be checked not assumed): entropy lowers s1 (truth is near one-hot, entropy 0.035 vs 0.446 predicted) but possibly for the wrong reason; TV does NOT help (predicted TV already below truth's: smoothing favours the collapsed solution); range_hinge and calc_l1 do nothing (inactive at the solution).
