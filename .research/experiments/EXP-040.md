# EXP-040 · Physical-phantom inversion of simulated COPD CT: U-Net predicting elemental-composition mass concentrations (arms A clean, C varied, D varied+conditioned) vs physics baselines

## Hypothesis

A 3D U-Net trained on cached simulated CT of 47 COPD patients predicts 8-channel composition concentration maps whose attenuation at 70 keV and at the UNSEEN 80 keV (computed from the prediction, never from the truth) is closer to the XCAT truth than (i) a 70-keV-only single-composition attenuation inverse and (ii) a 2-composition HU-threshold inverse, on 10 unseen patients.

## Motivation

EXP-039 recovered organ-group labels only. The goal is a physically interpretable phantom that can be passed through LEAP. Compositions are near-degenerate in attenuation shape, so the experiment must separate what intensity alone fixes from what anatomical priors supply.

## Method

Targets: per-voxel box-averaged mass concentration c_k (g/cm3) of the 8 distinct normalised elemental compositions (mc_id 3 and 6 are identical), built from native 0.25 mm uint8 phantoms + per-patient xlsx densities onto the existing 0.8 mm grid; closure check sum_k c_k kappa_k(E)/10 == simulator mu_mm. Baselines (physics, no learning except thresholds fitted on TRAIN patients): P0 70-keV-only single composition, P1 two-composition HU threshold, O1 oracle composition identity with density from mu70 (upper-bound diagnostic). Network: UNet3D (EXP-039 settings, 96^3 patches) with softplus 8-channel head; loss Huber(c)+L1(total density)+L1(mu70 from c); arms A clean-only, C varied (clean,n0_1e5,var1), D = C + FiLM; 2 seeds. Evaluation once on 10 test patients: concentration/density/mu70/mu80 errors per channel, closed-loop LEAP resimulation (predicted vs true phantom, same geometry) at 70 and 80 keV with noise-free deterministic response scored separately from noisy realisations. Frozen split configs/split_v1_frozen.json. See projects/p07_xcat_physical/PREREGISTRATION.md.

## Expected outcome

Network beats P0/P1 on bone and soft-tissue concentration and on mu80; soft-tissue sub-compositions (muscle/skin/aorta/lung tissue/air) stay poorly separated in concentration even if density is accurate because their attenuation curves coincide.

## Success criteria

Every channel and output scored (8 concentrations, total density, mu70, mu80, closed-loop HU at 70 and 80 keV). Detection floors from the baseline validation-patient spread are frozen in PREREGISTRATION.md BEFORE any network training. Primary: patient-level bootstrap CIs of paired differences NN-vs-best-baseline on mu80 MAE (HU-equivalent) and total-density MAE. Structural results (arithmetic: P0 reproduces mu70 exactly by construction; O1 mu70 exact) are labelled structural, not evidence. Conditioning (D-C) is interpreted only if it exceeds the seed spread and floor.

## Stop conditions

Closure check fails (>1e-4 relative) or targets misaligned with CT (>0.5 voxel): stop and repair before training. Non-finite loss: stop and report. No tuning on test patients; test evaluated once.

- **What worked:** Pooled in-distribution panel (10 inputs), body mu80 MAE: P1 32.5 HU, A 23.5, B 13.7, C 13.2, D 13.2; C-P1 -19.3 HU [-21.9,-17.0], 10/10 patients, far above the frozen floor 0.866 HU; body density MAE C 0.0132 vs P1 0.0373 g/cm3. Held-out e80 input: C 13.6 vs P1 27.9 HU. Closed loop: predicted phantom through LEAP reproduces the 70 keV CT (body MAE 6.3-7.7 HU, SSIM>=0.994) and the 80 keV CT the network never saw (6.2-7.5 HU vs 9.1 HU for reusing the 70 keV image); 80 keV error ~ 70 keV error.
- **What did not work:** Skin (support MAE 0.657 g/cm3) and blood/aorta (0.799) are not recovered by any arm (prediction ~0), as expected from the near-identical attenuation shape. Arm D (FiLM conditioning) fails on the unseen pitch 1.4 input: 142 HU / 0.143 g/cm3 (D-C +129.9 HU [126.9,133.0]). Overfit check stopped at step 1600/4000 (loss 0.078, not near zero). Preregistered note that mu70 has a ~15 HU floor for any voxelwise method was wrong for the network (6-8 HU against the phantom).
- **Interpretation:** Within this simulated, 10-patient scope the U-Net recovers a physically consistent phantom: it is much closer to the true concentrations/attenuation than 70-keV-only physics inverses, and the closed loop reproduces both the input CT and an unseen-energy CT. Noise-augmented training (B, C) is detected as better than clean-only (A) on noisy inputs; C-B and D-C are NOT detected (below floor/seed range), which is not evidence of no effect. Separation of air/lung/body/muscle/bone rests on anatomical priors, not CT physics; skin and blood are not separable. Conditioning offers no benefit and a harmful extrapolation on an unseen acquisition value.
- **Limitations:** Simulated data only; 10 test patients, 2 seeds; single phantom family (XCAT COPD) with targets from the generating phantoms; borrowed Duke1 geometry; orientation provisional; cross-energy tested only 70->80 keV; patients other than 11989 resimulated before the silent-FBP guard existed (values consistent, not rerun); no independent review yet.
- **Unresolved:** Independent review of the claim; whether anatomical priors transfer to real or DukeSim data; why B beats C on matched noise only slightly; whether D fails because of the conditioning range or FiLM design; cross-energy transfer at larger energy gaps.
- **Recommended next:** Independent review (different agent) attempting to break the pooled C-P1 claim and the closed-loop result; then decide whether a conditioning-range ablation (single factor) is warranted.
