## Motivation

Quantify forward-model mismatch against an independent simulator before any learning experiment (inverse-crime ladder rung L3-L4 inputs). Not tuning CTsynth to match XCIST.

projects/p04_xcist/generate.py builds 3 disk phantoms (water/Ca/I fractions) x {80,120 kVp} x {ref, 2x-subsampled, noisy} paired-ct records with XCIST 1.6.9 (1 row, 360 views, 900 cols, EI detector, no scatter). compare.py: stages M1 (XCIST ingredients, analytic chords), M1v (voxel chords), M2 (our xraylib kappa), M3 (ideal EI detector), M4 (our spectra_v1); metrics = RMS/mean of delta(-ln T) on rays with XCIST prep in (0.05,8); plus XCIST sub-sampling effect and Poisson variance law (var vs exp(prep), binned R2).
