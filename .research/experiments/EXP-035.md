# EXP-035 · Stage 0: prior profile test along the exact null direction and swap (deterministic, train split)

## Hypothesis

H0: a prior can only help if its penalty has its minimum at the truth along the exact null direction n and the swap; priors whose profile is minimised elsewhere (e.g. entropy on a uniform 0.5 start) cannot select the truth whatever the optimiser does.

## Motivation


## Method

Deterministic, noise-free, on 20 TRAIN-split cases (prior hyperparameters are chosen here, never on validation). For each candidate prior R with weight lambda in a log grid: total(t)=proj_MSE(truth+t*d)+lambda*R(truth+t*d), d in {exact null n, swap, random smooth A/F field, wrong-label (A<->F swapped truth)}. Priors: P1 calcification absence (L1 and L0-surrogate of calcification outside a sparse support, plus hard calc=0 parameterisation); P2 bimodality/entropy of the A/F split in tissue; P3 piecewise-constancy (TV of the adipose fraction, tissue only); P4 train-split marginal histogram of the A/F fraction (sliced Wasserstein) = WEAK SUPERVISION from train labels, labelled as such and never counted as label-free. Output: argmin_t, margin total(t)-total(0), and the lambda window [lambda_min where the prior beats the data curvature along n (4e-5 RMSE/unit => MSE 1.6e-9 t^2), lambda_max where it distorts the truth along identifiable directions]. Truth-profile uses the exact float64 null vector.

## Expected outcome


## Success criteria

Per prior: report (not pass/fail on one number) argmin and margin along n, swap and wrong-label, and the lambda window. A prior proceeds to Stage 1/2 only if argmin is within |t|<=0.02 of the truth along n AND the wrong-label profile has a margin >= 10x numerical resolution above the truth, over a non-empty lambda window. Detection floor stated: sensitivity along n is 4e-5 RMSE/unit (floor 1.3e-8), along swap 1.3e-2/unit, so a trained network needs projection RMSE <= ~3e-6 to resolve |t|=0.1 along n, but only <= 6e-4 to resolve a swap of the same size.

## Stop conditions

Any use of validation or test cases; any label in a non-flagged prior; nonfinite values.

## Known limitations


## Notes

Amendments after delta review T-071 (made before results were read): (1) P1 (calcification absence) has its minimum at the truth along n by construction (truth calcification ~0), so its gate component cannot fail; its informative content is lam_max (distortion of real lesions along the calc-scale direction) and the margin vs optimiser residual. (2) P2/P3 are symmetric under A<->F; wrong-label margins are reported as separate data (mse) and prior (dR) terms; any margin for them comes from the data term. (3) Numerical resolution is defined as the optimiser residual MSE: 2e-8 (best label-free refinement, EXP-028) and 1e-6 (physics-only networks); the t grid step is 0.02 (margins are read at |t|=0.1, not at the argmin, so the step does not set the gate). (4) lambda windows are chosen with TRAIN labels (profile uses truth), so later claims must not say label-free for the weight choice. (5) The A<->F swap/wrong-label here is the uniform swap; the null-compensated large flip (t up to ~1.4 along n) is not covered by the +-0.4 grid and will be added only if the results make it decisive. Smoke run RUN-0092 (1 case) preceded the registered run RUN-0093 (20 train cases).
