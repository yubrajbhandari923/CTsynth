# EXP-004 · EXP-005 B0-B4 ladder: does any prior select the true member of the ambiguous family?

## Hypothesis

PRE-REGISTERED, derived from F-018/F-019 (deterministic proofs, not guesses). On synthetic AAPM-schema data, 3 materials from 2 spectral channels, image domain: (P1) B3 (label-free, consistency only, LINEAR output head) reaches near-zero consistency loss while its material RMSE stays LARGE and its error vector aligns with the F-014 null direction [-0.723,+0.691,-0.008] (|cos| > 0.9 on tissue pixels): low loss, wrong materials. (P2) B3's material error varies substantially across random seeds (relative spread of tissue RMSE > 20%) because the implicit prior, not the data, selects the solution; its CONSISTENCY loss by contrast is nearly seed-independent (< 5%). (P3) Adding sum_to_one (B4) collapses the ambiguity: tissue-pixel RMSE drops by >5x versus B3-linear and seed spread shrinks. (P4) nonneg alone (softplus head) does NOT achieve that collapse (tissue RMSE within 2x of B3-linear), per F-019. (P5) A softmax head, which silently imposes sum-to-one EVERYWHERE, improves tissue pixels but is WRONG on air: its air-pixel error exceeds that of the softmax_with_air head. (P6) ENTROPY HYPOTHESIS (testable, direction not assumed): entropy minimisation reduces the number of mixed voxels; we measure mixed-voxel fraction and boundary-band RMSE and report whichever direction occurs. (P7) B2 supervised beats every label-free variant on material RMSE, establishing the labelled upper reference.

## Motivation

F-018 proves consistency alone cannot identify 3-from-2. This experiment measures empirically WHICH prior does the identifying work, and whether B3 fails in the predicted direction.

## Method

Synthetic generator mirroring the AAPM schema with the real CC-BY spectra/mu tables; image-domain effective-mu observation model (no fan-beam projector exists, so sinogram-domain is out of scope). Rungs: B0 classical simplex, B1 classical TV, B2 supervised U-Net (labels), B3 label-free U-Net consistency-only with linear head, B4 variants = consistency + one prior at a time (nonneg/softplus head, sum_to_one, tv, entropy) and the softmax and softmax_with_air heads. Matched optimisation budgets across all learned rungs (same epochs, lr, batch size, identical U-Net size). 3 seeds per learned configuration. Hyperparameters and early stopping use VALIDATION CONSISTENCY ONLY for label-free rungs (no ground truth anywhere in that path: structurally enforced, train_label_free takes no truth argument); the supervised rung may use validation labels and this is disclosed. Test split untouched. Metrics: official s1/s2, per-material RMSE, regime-separated (air/tissue/band) RMSE, consistency loss, null-direction alignment |cos| of the error with the F-014 vector, mixed-voxel fraction, seed spread.
