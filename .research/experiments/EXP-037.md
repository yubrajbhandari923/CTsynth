# EXP-037 · Stage 2: network prior study (structural calcification/sum constraints and simple priors vs baseline and wrong-prior controls)

## Hypothesis

H2: if Stage 1 shows calcification/sum knowledge identifies the split, a network with the same information supplied (P1 structural: occupancy sigmoid x 2-class A/F softmax + separate sparse calcification head, support from images; P1 soft: calcification L1 at the Stage-0 window) recovers it from physics alone, whereas bimodality (P2), TV (P3) alone do not select the truth (they sharpen whichever side starts ahead). The weights are set by the Stage-0 window, not by 10% of the collapsed-solution loss (EXP-025).

## Method

EXP-023 recipe exactly (800 train/100 val, depth4 width32, lr 3e-4 warmup 300 clip 1, 6000 steps, batch 4, 32 views, validation only), 3 seeds per arm, baseline reused (RUN-0047/48/49). Arms: P1s structural; P1l calcification L1 soft (2 weights from the window); P2 entropy/bimodality (1 weight); P3 TV(a) (1 weight); controls: C1 wrong-prior (P1 with calcification forced present at the swapped fraction / P4 histogram mirrored), C2 prior-only (no data term; shows what the prior alone picks), C3 P1s with oracle-labelled support (support from truth; upper bound, labelled). Optional P4 (train-marginal Wasserstein) only as a flagged weak-supervision arm. Metrics per arm/seed on the 100 val cases: s1, s1_swapped, s2, boundary RMSE, null- and swap-coordinate error (exact n), tissue |S-1|, calcification mass outside truth support, projection RMSE (100 cases), collapse flag (adipose std<0.05), dead-gradient fraction (grad<1e-8), max grad norm, seed range. Runs gated: waves of 3 seeds, synthesize each wave before the next.

## Success criteria

An arm 'helps' only if (i) 3-seed mean s1 < 0.2956 - max(2x pooled seed range, 0.05) and >=2/3 seeds s1 < 0.15, (ii) the wrong-prior control C1 and prior-only C2 are NOT equally good, (iii) not attributable to optimisation (Stage-1 ordering agrees; dead-gradient fraction < 0.5). Detection floor: baseline seed range 0.0165 => differences below ~0.035 are undetectable with 3 seeds; with 1-2/3 successes extend that arm to 5 seeds before any claim (3 seeds cannot separate a 1/3 success rate from 0 or 1/2). Positive findings stay preliminary until independent review.

## Stop conditions

Stage 0 or Stage 1 gate not passed; labels in any non-flagged arm; dead-gradient fraction > 0.5 in >=2 seeds of an arm (report as optimisation failure, do not tune the arm); >3 failed runs without review; total beyond 24 runs.
