Like Hellwege it is 2-from-2 (determined, no sum-to-one row) and so says nothing about our 3-from-2 null space. - F-018 [supported, medium] EXPERIMENT A (deterministic, no training): a pure measurement-consistency loss CANNOT identify 3 material maps from 2 spectral measurements on the AAPM model, and this is not a linearisation artifact. Experiment A proves (machine precision, real polychromatic model) that measurement consistency alone has a continuum of exact global minima for 3-from-2, so B3 cannot be expected to recover materials and its purpose is to demonstrate this empirically and to reveal WHICH member of the ambiguous family the network's implicit priors select. Neither primary paper is a precedent: Hellwege is 2-from-2 determined with no regulariser, Peng is 2-from-2 with an unpaired-reference adversarial prior. No faithful reproduction of either will be attempted on the 3-material task: it would be a category error, and both papers' complete methods are in any case unverifiable (no code, unstated activation/bin count for Hellwege, supplement unavailable for Peng). The scientific question becomes: which added prior selects the true member, and does it do so for a physical reason or by accident?
- D-005 (2026-10-11) AAPM benchmark design is governed by two facts established in discovery: (1) the challenge data are NOISELESS and the forward model was shared with all participants, so this is an EXACT-RECOVERY problem (winning s1 RMSE 6.8e-7, i.e. ~1e-6 is reachable and is NOT a noise floor) - a denoising-style evaluation would be meaningless; (2) 3-material recovery from 2 spectral measurements is made well posed by a SUM-TO-ONE constraint on VOLUME FRACTIONS (2 spectral equations + 1 partition constraint = 3 equations, unique when the three (mu_low, mu_high) points are non-collinear), not by nonnegativity or priors. Therefore: the classical baseline uses volume fractions with an explicit simplex constraint; nonnegativity alone is insufficient; and our metric must match the published s1 = mean over cases of sqrt(||t-r||^2/(3n)) on dimensionless volume fractions.
