# Pre-registration: inverse U-Net arms A-D (COPD XCAT, simulated CT) - FROZEN before any training

Frozen on 2026-10-29 (before the 65 additional phantoms were simulated and before any model was trained). Files frozen with it: `configs/split_v1_frozen.json` (sha256 of the id lists `3417bfcb...c708f`), `configs/dataset_plan_v1.json`. Changing any of them after training has started voids the pre-registration; deviations are reported as deviations.

**Question.** How accurately does a 3D U-Net recover XCAT organ-group labels from simulated CT of UNSEEN patients; how does accuracy change with noise, reconstruction kernel and acquisition settings; does conditioning on the acquisition metadata help beyond training on varied data?

**Data.** 67 COPD phantoms (all on DRCC after the verified transfer), 0.8 mm grid, borrowed Duke1 helical geometry (not the phantoms' original acquisition), voxel size 0.25 mm assumed. Split by patient: 47 train / 10 val / 10 test, seed 0; copd_22728 and copd_21535 (used to develop and check the simulator) are forced into TRAIN, so they cannot appear in the test set. Variants: train and val patients get `clean`, `n0_1e5` and one varied draw `var1` per patient (log-uniform N0 in [3e4, 1e6], LEAP low-pass W in {1,1,2,4}, 60 or 70 keV, 1 or 2 views-decimation; pitch 1.0). Test patients additionally get `n0_1e6, n0_3e5, n0_3e4, lowpass_w2, lowpass_w4, dec2, e60` (in-distribution KINDS of change) and the HELD-OUT acquisition settings `e80` (80 keV) and `pitch1p4` (pitch 1.4), which no arm ever trains on. N0 is photons per ray per view (not a calibrated dose).

**Arms (identical split, identical optimiser budget of 40 000 steps, batch 4 patches of 96^3, 2 seeds each).** A: clean only. B: noisy `n0_1e5` only. C: `clean`, `n0_1e5`, `var1`. D: same data as C plus FiLM conditioning on the 7-number acquisition vector. Network: 3D U-Net (base 16, depth 3, 1.4 M parameters); loss: cross-entropy + soft Dice; AdamW, lr 2e-3 cosine, bf16, class-balanced patch sampling (70% foreground-centred). **No hyper-parameter search**: these are the defaults fixed here; the only selection is the checkpoint with the best validation macro Dice (validation patients, the arm's own training variants, non-overlapping windows). The test patients are touched once, after training finishes.

**Primary outcome.** Macro Dice over the 14 foreground classes of each test patient's pooled voxels (classes absent from both truth and prediction skipped), averaged over the 10 test patients and over the 2 seeds; 95% bootstrap CI over test patients (4000 draws, paired for differences). **Comparisons:** (1) B-A and C-B and C-A on every variant; (2) D-C (conditioning); (3) all arms vs the intensity-only baseline (per-class Gaussian on HU fitted on the training patients' clean CTs); (4) in-distribution panel mean versus the two held-out settings (acquisition generalisation, separate from patient generalisation).

**Detection floor (stated before running).** With 10 test patients the paired bootstrap can only resolve differences of roughly 0.02-0.03 macro Dice; a smaller difference is "not detected", not "no effect". Seed spread (2 seeds) is reported next to every arm mean; an arm difference smaller than the seed spread is not interpreted. Stop rules: a run whose training loss is non-finite is stopped and reported; non-convergence (validation macro Dice not above the intensity baseline's validation value) is reported as such, not tuned away.

**Not claimed.** Segmentation of simulated CT says nothing about recovery of physical material composition, about real scanner data, or about other noise models (no scatter, electronic noise or beam hardening); organ-group labels follow the xlsx names only; orientation letters are provisional; the simulator's geometry is borrowed.
