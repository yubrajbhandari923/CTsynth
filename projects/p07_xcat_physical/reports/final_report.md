# Physical-phantom inversion of simulated COPD CT with a 3D U-Net (EXP-040)

Simulated data only (XCAT COPD phantoms through our LEAP helical simulator, borrowed Duke1 geometry, monoenergetic, no scatter). 10 held-out test patients scored once, 2 seeds per arm. Preliminary until an independent review (CLAUDE.md promotion gate). Pre-registration with frozen floors: `PREREGISTRATION.md`.

## What was built
- **Target** (not the 15 semantic classes): per-voxel mass concentration c_k (g/cm³) of the 8 distinct elemental compositions of the XCAT tables. μ(E) = Σ c_k (μ/ρ)_k(E) reproduces the simulator's μ at 70 and 80 keV to ≤5.7e-7 relative on all 67 patients (`material_audit.md`).
- **Network** `PhysUNet` (UNet3D base 16, depth 3, softplus head), 70 keV CT in, 8 concentration maps out. Loss: Huber on concentrations + total density + μ70 consistency; no Dice; 80 keV not in the loss.
- **Arms** (same budget as EXP-039, 40 000 steps, batch 4×96³): A clean only; B n0=1e5 noisy only; C clean + n0_1e5 + var1; D = C + FiLM on the acquisition vector. Seeds 0, 1.
- **Baselines** (70 keV image only): P0 one composition (muscle); P1 bone/soft split at 125 HU (fitted on train); O1 oracle true composition identity per voxel (diagnostic, uses ground truth).
- **Closed loop**: predicted concentrations → μ(70) and μ(80) → LEAP forward projection + helical FBP, compared with the same pipeline on the true phantom.

## Validation addendum (2026-10-30): corrected closed loop, pitch-1.4 cause, material recovery
Original (preliminary) results above are preserved; corrections are listed here. Provenance: model, training and prediction code are byte-identical on the cluster and at commit `f9cbcc2` (sha256 comparison of 16 files; unchanged since); the evaluation code is the current commit. Output directories: `resim_v1_preliminary/` (kept), `resim_v2/` (corrected).

### 1. All ten patients, corrected closed loop (`resim_v2`)
Guards added (`assert_valid_concentrations`, `assert_valid_ct`; unit-tested): predicted concentrations must be finite, in [0, 8] g/cm³ and on the true grid; the true-phantom 70 keV recon must match the cached CT to <1 HU; the 80 keV truth must be finite and within 60 HU of 70 keV; every predicted-phantom CT must be within 100 HU of the true-phantom CT (coupled noise: 300 HU); LEAP allocation messages are counted and must be zero. Result: **10/10 patients passed, 0 LEAP allocation failures, truth70 vs cached CT 0.064–0.068 HU body MAE for every patient.** Root cause of the earlier failure: LEAP allocates device memory outside PyTorch's caching allocator; with two 8-channel volumes, projections and PyTorch's cache resident, the FBP `cudaMalloc` failed non-fatally and left an unreconstructed volume (860 HU). Fix: truth/cached CTs on the CPU, concentration volumes freed before projection, `empty_cache()` before every projection and FBP, plus the guards. Peak PyTorch allocation 11.9–16.3 GB of the 23.6 GB card (`peak_torch_alloc_MiB` in each `resim_p*.json`). **Conclusion unchanged:** v2 equals v1 for the nine patients that had been valid, and for 11989 equals the earlier repaired rerun.

Closed loop, body MAE in HU (mean over 10 patients and 2 seeds; deterministic): reproduction at 70 keV A 6.30, B 7.18, C 7.67, D 7.48; at the unseen 80 keV A 6.16, B 7.08, C 7.52, D 7.20; trivial 'reuse the 70 keV image' 9.11. Paired patient-bootstrap difference (80 keV predicted-phantom CT minus reuse reference): A −2.95 [−3.57, −2.33] (10/10 patients), B −2.03 [−2.67, −1.34] (10/10), C −1.60 [−2.40, −0.69] (**8/10**; worse for 13799, +0.27, and 23235, +1.22), D −1.91 [−2.57, −1.17] (9/10). The 80 keV error is not larger than the 70 keV reproduction error (−0.10 to −0.28 HU). Per-patient values: `test_v2/resim_per_patient.csv`; per-patient material/density tables: `validation/per_patient_tables.txt`.
The 70→80 keV transfer test is therefore passed with a small margin (the reference is already near 9 HU because soft-tissue HU changes little between 70 and 80 keV). Leakage audit: the concentration targets are energy-independent; the loss and validation selection use only the 70 keV attenuation (`kappa70`, `train_phys.py`); training inputs are 70 keV (clean, n0_1e5) and, for arms C and D, the `var1` variant at **60 keV**; no 80 keV image or 80 keV attenuation enters any arm. Caveat: the held-out energy is 80 keV only; the `e60` test input is not held out for arms C and D (60 keV is in `var1`).

### 1b. What the C − P1 gap does and does not show (independent-review correction)
The pooled 19.3 HU lead over P1 is **mostly image restoration, not composition or energy inference**: P1 reads μ70 straight from the noisy or blurred CT and inherits its error, whereas the network is scored against the phantom and learns to undo noise and blur. Evidence: the gap is already −19.5 HU at μ70, where no energy extrapolation is involved; on the clean input (pre-registered primary) C − P1 is −5.09 HU at μ70 and −7.46 HU [−8.14, −6.65] at μ80 (10/10 patients), so the energy-dependence part is at most ≈2.4 HU; on clean input C − A is +1.90 HU [+1.48, +2.33] (A, trained on clean images, is better for all 10 patients). O1 is not an upper bound for the network because it too reads μ70 from the CT (15.7 HU on clean input; C beats it by 19.7 HU pooled). Closed-loop numbers come from **clean-input predictions only** (the stored predictions are for the clean variant). The 'reuse the 70 keV image' reference uses the true phantom's 70 keV CT, so it measures a different error source than the network's 80 keV error; the transfer evidence is that the network's 80 keV error does not exceed its own 70 keV reproduction error (−0.10 to −0.28 HU) and, for arm C (the headline arm, 7.67 HU at 70 keV and 7.52 HU at 80 keV), is below the reference in 8 of 10 patients. Arm C is the worst arm in the closed loop (A 6.30/6.16, B 7.18/7.08, D 7.48/7.20 HU at 70/80 keV).
Noise-vs-clean contrasts are C − A and B − A (detected, large on noisy inputs); C − B is 'varied vs noisy-only' and is not detected.

### 2. Pitch-1.4 failure of arm D: cause and verdict
Diagnostic (job 203568, **validation patients only**, frozen D_s0 and D_s1, 4 patients × clean/n0_1e5/var1, no test data; `validation/cond_diag_val.json`): overriding only the pitch entry of the conditioning vector from 1.0 to 1.4 raises body μ80 error from 10–16 HU to 133–150 HU per case (141–143 HU as per-seed means; density MAE 0.010–0.017 → 0.140–0.144 g/cm³) on every validation case and both seeds; setting the energy entry to 80/70 changes it by at most 3.0 HU; restoring pitch=1.0 reproduces the nominal values. Hence the image, geometry, reconstruction sampling and target alignment are not the cause (the same pitch-1.4 test images give 12.4 HU with arm C). Mechanism: pitch was constant 1.0 in all training variants (`cond[4]`; the `apod` entry is also always 0); for a constant-1 input the FiLM weight column receives the same gradient as the bias, so the learnt weight equals the bias (summed |weights| for the pitch entry 65.75 vs summed |bias| 65.75 for seed 0, 70.00 vs 70.00 for seed 1). With w = b the learnt pitch-plus-bias constant is 2b at pitch 1.0 and 1.4b + b = 2.4b at pitch 1.4, i.e. the FiLM modulation is 1.2× its trained value (before the network's nonlinear amplification). Conditioning metadata is identical in training and inference (both read `meta["cond"]`). **Verdict: not an implementation or metadata bug; a deterministic extrapolation failure of un-centred, zero-variance conditioning features (an encoding/architecture design flaw exposed by distribution shift).** No bug-fix regression test applies; the failure is documented, not repaired, and I make no claim that conditioning helps. A fix (centre/scale features or drop zero-variance entries) needs retraining, which was out of scope. On the test set D's pitch-1.4 μ80 error is 133–149 HU for all ten patients (C: 10–14 HU).

### 3. Material recovery versus CT reproducibility (test patients, clean input)
`validation/material_recovery_test.json` (job 203570). Mean over patients of the predicted mass assigned to each channel inside voxels whose true dominant channel is the row (each row is normalised by the dominant channel's own mass, so off-diagonal entries include genuine partial-volume neighbours, not only misassignment) (arm C seeds 0/1): body → body 0.97–0.99; muscle → muscle 0.98; lung tissue → lung 0.97–0.98; flat bone (`sternum`) → 0.92–0.95, with ~5 % to muscle and 2–6 % to spine bone; spine bone → 0.88–0.94 (4–9 % to flat bone); **skin → muscle 0.56–0.60 and body 0.42–0.45 (skin channel ≈ 0.00); blood (aorta) → muscle 0.87–0.94 (aorta channel ≈ 0.00–0.01)**. Predicted total mass per channel over the valid region divided by true mass (seed 0): body 1.005, flat bone 1.002, muscle 1.087, lung 1.061, spine bone 1.003 (arm A 1.119), skin 0.000, blood 0.002. Skin and blood are therefore not recovered by arm C (skin→skin 0.00/0.003, blood→blood 0.00/0.007 for seeds 0/1; arm D seed 1 recovers 32 % of skin mass as skin, so skin is not strictly non-separable, whereas blood is not recovered by any arm); their mass is assigned to attenuation-equivalent neighbours, which is why the CT is reproduced (the merged soft group {muscle, lung tissue, skin, blood} has support MAE 0.039 g/cm³ for C versus 0.026 for A on clean input) while the channel identity is wrong. ## Invalidated assumptions and deviations (documented, not hidden)
1. **"μ70 error of any voxelwise method has a floor of ≈15.3 HU" (PREREGISTRATION §7 note) was wrong for the network.** P0/P1/O1 inherit the 15.3 HU CT-vs-phantom discrepancy because they read μ70 from the CT. The network does not: it outputs a phantom, and its error against the phantom is 6–8 HU on clean input, i.e. it is closer to the phantom than the CT is. Interpretation (not tested here): the CT-vs-phantom discrepancy is a systematic function of anatomy and reconstruction blur that the network can learn. The floors (§7) were frozen from the baselines and remain valid for the comparisons made; this note only corrects the interpretation of the baseline μ70 value.
2. Resimulation had CUDA out-of-memory failures on the 24 GB GPUs for large patients. Fixes (truth/cached CTs on CPU, free concentration volumes before projection, abort on silent FBP failure) are committed. **Patient 11989 first produced a silent invalid FBP (860 HU error in all 8 arms; LEAP printed a non-fatal cudaMalloc error); the result was discarded (kept as `resim_p11989.FAILED_fbp_oom.json.bak`) and the patient rerun alone.** **Update: all ten patients were rerun with the guarded code (`resim_v2`); the nine others reproduced the earlier values exactly (max difference 0.0 HU over 160 patient-run values).**
3. The long single-patient overfit check (Stage A) was stopped at step 1600 of 4000 (loss 0.078, still falling), so "overfit to near zero" was not demonstrated. Gradient finiteness, checkpoint round trip, resume and export round trip passed.
4. Cross-energy transfer was tested only for 70 → 80 keV.
5. Seed ranges use 2 seeds. B and D have seed-to-seed variation in validation selection score (e.g. B: 0.067 vs 0.096), so arm differences smaller than the seed range are reported as not detected.

## Protocol deviations found in the independent review
- Arms C and D were selected on validation using only the clean and n0_1e5 variants (`train_phys.py` line 101), not on their own `var1` variant as PREREGISTRATION §3 might be read to imply; validation patients only, no test data.
- The PREREGISTRATION commit post-dates the start of training (floors were computed from validation baselines before training; the commit came after).
- The baselines (`eval_baselines.py`) treat the `e60` and `var1` inputs as if they were 70 keV images, which adds roughly 1 HU to the pooled gap.
- 'Zero LEAP allocation failures' is a printed count in `resim_job.sh`, not an enforced guard; the enforced guards are the CT/concentration assertions.
- RUN-0118 is a backfilled manual record mixing test and validation metrics (partition declared per binding); RUN-0117 is historical and unmodified.

## Limitations
- Simulated data; borrowed Duke1 geometry; 10 test patients drawn from 67 phantoms of one phantom family; native voxel 0.25 mm assumed; orientation provisional (`axcodes` convention, check in Slicer).
- The 8 compositions are not identifiable from CT intensities (two dominant singular values); the network separates air/lung/body/muscle/bone by anatomical context, and cannot separate skin or blood from soft tissue (measured above).
- Targets come from the same phantoms that generated the CT, so there is no label noise; this overstates what real data would permit.
- No independent review yet (F-049-style); finding stays preliminary.
- `origin/feature/xcat-simulator-clean` still contains six mistaken EXP-036 commits; do not use it.
